Dough & Ferment Calculators

Bread, yogurt and lacto-ferments in one Android app. Works offline.

Get it on Google Play

The iPhone app is coming soon

Until it is in the App Store, you can put the calculator on your Home Screen from Safari. It opens full screen like an app and works offline.

Add it to your Home Screen

  1. Open bread.sourdoughcalculator.uk in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Share button, the square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down and choose “Add to Home Screen”, then tap Add.
  4. Open it from the new icon on your Home Screen.

How these work

  • Each calculator is its own app. Add it to your home screen and it works offline.
  • Weather and location lookups need a connection, and only run when you ask.
  • No accounts. Recipes, starter logs and settings stay on your device.
  • Each app keeps its own settings, so units and location are set once per app.
