How these work

  • Each calculator is its own app. Add it to your home screen and it works offline.
  • Weather and location lookups need a connection, and only run when you ask.
  • No accounts. Recipes, starter logs and settings stay on your device.
  • Each app keeps its own settings, so units and location are set once per app.
