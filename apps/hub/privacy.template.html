Two things share this page: the web calculators at the addresses on the home page, and the Dough & Ferment Calculators app for Android. Each has its own section below.

The web calculators

Bread, yogurt, lacto-ferment, and pasta & noodles, each at its own address.

The short version

There are no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Nothing you enter is sent to the people who made these apps. What you type and save stays in your browser on your device.

What is stored on your device

Each calculator keeps its settings, your last inputs and, where it has them, your history (saved loaves, the starter feeding log, past ferments) in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser's site data for that calculator removes it. Each calculator is on its own address, so they do not share this.

What is sent, and only when you ask

The calculators work without sending anything. A few things use a service outside the app, and only when you tap the button for them (or switch on "detect automatically"):

  • Weather. "Use my location" asks your browser for your location. Your position, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to read the outdoor temperature and forecast, and in the bread app also the last three weeks of temperatures to estimate your tap water.
  • Your town, for water hardness. In the bread and lacto-ferment apps the same rounded position is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) to find your town, so the app can look up water hardness. A UK postcode typed into Settings is looked up inside the app and is not sent anywhere.
  • Google Calendar. The lacto-ferment app's calendar buttons are links. Tapping one opens Google Calendar with the batch details in the link, and Google handles it from there.

Those services have their own privacy policies and may see your IP address, as any website does. The apps do not send them anything else.

Hosting

The pages are served by Cloudflare, which, like any host, sees the ordinary request details needed to deliver a web page. The apps add no cookies and no tracking scripts.
