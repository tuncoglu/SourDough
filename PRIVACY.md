# Privacy

The full privacy policy for Dough & Ferment Calculators, the Android app and the websites at
sourdoughcalculator.uk, is in [SourDoughMobile/PRIVACY.md](SourDoughMobile/PRIVACY.md).
It is also published at https://sourdoughcalculator.uk/privacy.

In short: no accounts, no analytics, and everything you save stays on your
device or in your browser. With your permission, your coordinates go to
Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap's Nominatim to find your local temperature and
area, and only when you tap the button for it.

## Ask the baker (not currently available)

"Ask the baker" was an optional box for questions about the lazy sourdough
method, answered by an AI service. It is switched off: it does not appear in
the apps, and the websites no longer run the service it needs, so nothing is
sent anywhere for it. If it ever returns, the policy will be updated first to
say exactly what would be sent and to whom.
